## What is the end-to-end argument, with timestamps?

### Takeaway

Her argument is an inversion: the model is no longer the bottleneck; the engineer’s job is to state intent, build the environment, and give the agent a way to check its own work. The Michelin kitchen is the picture of that job. Volume (she cites roughly 2,500 merged production PRs in a month on this stream) is a by-product, and a lot of those PRs are gardening, not features. The host supplies the expo/pass image, the one-way-door challenge, and the “dimmer switch” against Karpathy-style vibe coding.

### Inferences

- The talk is one argument in three movements: (1) trust is earned by verification and a prepared environment, not by a better prompt; (2) the kitchen metaphor is how you scale that from one agent to many restaurants without dropping craft; (3) the ceiling is verifiability, which she states rather than hand-waves.
- “Dark” on this stream means agents merge while she sleeps, and she still samples the commit history. The host’s dimmer is a partial dark factory, not lights-out with nobody ever reading code. Matt Pocock’s later public dictionary defines a dark factory more strictly (no human reads the code that comes out). ## What concrete harness or workflow practices does she describe?

### Takeaway

On this stream the concrete system is: a verification skill with a small Playwright/CDP CLI, a narrow codebase (Dune, lint, types, feature directories), skills that are markdown process, transcript mining, an outer loop (Grok Bot plus Slack/Linear/X) feeding Cursor project coordinators, and full autopilot that fuzzes each PR with verifier agents before merge. She still samples landed commits and reads code for gardening. She does not, on this recording, walk through CLAUDE.md or a named CI vendor.

### Cited Findings

Every Cursor or xAI app she mentions has an auto-maintained verification skill (19:10). Full autopilot spawns verifier agents per PR, fuzzes by clicking around, fixes regressions, then lands; tunable from ten verifiers to one; token-heavy (52:50). — [PostGrok source](https://postgrok.com/sources/2026-10-05-youtube-poteto-software-factory)

- Her rejection, caption-derived, at 11:20: “I’ve never really liked the term software factory.
